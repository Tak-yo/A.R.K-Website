About A.R.K Initiative

A.R.K Initiative is a youth-led groundup dedicated to wildlife conservation and the protection of animal rights. Inspired by the biblical Noah's Ark, we embody three core objectives: Wildlife Conservation, Animal Welfare, and Restoration of ecosystems.

We believe that protecting Singapore's incredible biodiversity requires collaboration across disciplines and communities. Through education, advocacy, and hands-on conservation work, we're building a movement of nature lovers and environmental stewards.

Ready to Make a Difference?

Join our community of nature lovers and conservation advocates. Whether you're interested in volunteering, attending events, or simply learning more, there's a place for you in A.R.K Initiative.
